A story game for ages 7–12 (NASA Space Apps 2026 · "Build a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer").
Runs in any browser. No download, no account (several players can each keep their own astronaut and progress on one device). English, fully voiced.

## What's inside
- `index.html` + `src/` — the game (Phaser 3.90 from `lib/`, no CDN for code)
  - `core.js` language, local player profiles (localStorage), procedural music + sound effects, voice-line playback
  - `ui.js` dialogue, choice cards with costs, NASA cards, settings
  - `title.js` key art → who's playing (local profiles) → astronaut creator (call sign, face, suit colour) → chapters with "continue where you left off"
  - `scenes.js` Phaser scenes: cinematic landing, Mars landscape, Wake-the-base cable puzzle, greenhouse
  - `story.js` chapter scripts
  - `sim.js` Earth–Mars distance and light delay from NASA JPL Keplerian elements; resource model
- `data/text.json` all text · `data/cards.json` NASA science cards with sources · `data/game.json` sources and rules
- `data/donki_latest.json` daily NASA DONKI snapshot (written by `.github/workflows/donki-snapshot.yml`)
- `nasa.html` "NASA data we used" page (live light delay + DONKI snapshot + every source)
- `assets/voice/en/` all voice lines (mp3) + `index.json`; numbers for the live light delay are stitched in at runtime
- `tools/art/` scripts that draw all scene art (SVG → PNG); `tests/balance.mjs` balance simulation

## Art credits
The painted backgrounds (`assets/bg/`), crew portraits and the full-body astronaut were generated by the team with an AI image tool and then edited (upscaled, cut out, head swaps, sky mask).
The landing rocket is cut out of the team's landing painting (the empty pad was inpainted); its legs are drawn in code so they can unfold. All lighting, weather, day/night and particle effects are made in code at runtime.

## Voice credits
All character voices are synthetic, generated before the event. No real person's voice was recorded, and none of the team members' voices are used.
- English: Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) via kokoro-onnx. Voices af_bella (NOVA), af_heart (Aylin), am_puck (Deniz), am_michael (Kerem), bm_george (Mission Control).

## New in the final version
- Hands-on mini-games inside the chapters (`src/minis.js`): oxygen valve, plant care, fold the panels, aim the dish at Earth,
  crew into the shelter, rover dash. Each score changes the chapter's air, power or dose.
- Voiced "Why?" moments inside the chapters: NOVA explains a real fact while a card shows its source
  (Mars Trek, MOXIE, Curiosity RAD, Opportunity's dust, NASA OCHMO shelter data, ISS / RadLab dose comparison).
- Chapter 5 asks NASA DONKI directly first (api.nasa.gov, then NASA CCMC) and falls back to the daily snapshot, always labelled,
  then lets kids pick the crew's job for the day (EVA, exercise, plant experiment, machine check) based on the real Sun.
- CSA resources for teachers on `nasa.html`. (`src/mission.js` keeps the source list; its older 10-sol mode is no longer in the menu.)
- Sprint reports: `sprint-reports/`.

## Use of AI (Super Intelligence)
- **Images:** the backgrounds and characters were generated with an AI image tool and then edited by the team.
  Every game screen shows a visible "AI-generated art · synthetic voices" watermark, and the slides mark AI images too.
- **Audio:** all voices are synthetic (Kokoro-82M, Apache-2.0, via kokoro-onnx); this is also written in each MP3's
  ID3 metadata (artist and comment tags). Music and sound effects are synthesized in code at runtime (Web Audio).
- **Data:** no number is AI-generated. Every number has a cited source or is labelled "game estimate".
